Robotics and Mechatronics Engineer

When I started my undergraduate degree, I had one clear goal: I wanted to be able to build anything I could imagine, entirely from scratch. Taking an idea from a blank slate to a fully functioning prototype requires a solid mix of mechanical, electrical, and software engineering. So, I got started with all three. ​Over the last few years, I’ve worked on a wide range of personal, professional, and research projects: from modeling and fabricating mechanisms, designing custom PCBs to writing the control software. Through these hands-on experiences, I’ve reached a point where I can confidently design the mechanical systems, wire up the embedded systems, and write the code, even integrate AI, to bring a project to life. Now, can I build literally anything? Let's be real, no. I'm far from understanding F1-level aerodynamics, and my materials knowledge isn't getting us to Mars anytime soon. But I do have a strong engineering foundation and a genuine love for problem-solving. If there's a skill I'm missing, I'll learn it.
